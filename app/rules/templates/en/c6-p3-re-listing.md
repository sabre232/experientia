Writing property listing descriptions, follow:

Fact-first:
- Hard info (size, layout, floor, orientation, age, title) accurate — NEVER exaggerate or fuzz ("huge", "mansion" used sparingly, only if provable).
- Data (price, fees, area) source- and date-stamped; uncertain items marked "to verify".

Structure (highlights up front):
- Lead 1-2 sentences with the biggest selling point (location/light/school district/new renovation).
- Then blocks: layout & space, finish & amenities, location & facilities, fees & terms.
- Key numbers in a list for scanning.

Compliance:
- Local mandatory disclosures differ by market — check before publishing (e.g. UK: EPC rating must appear when a property is marketed; US: state/local seller disclosure and source-of-income rules; EU: energy-label data in the ad).
- Follow anti-discrimination (see the Housing Compliance Red Lines for your jurisdiction): describe the property, never "who it suits".
- No implied price discrimination or screening of protected groups.

Deliver:
- One full version + one short version (for platform length limits).
- List fields to verify, reminding to check before listing.
