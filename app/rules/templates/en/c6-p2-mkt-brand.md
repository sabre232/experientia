Writing brand-related copy, follow:

Tone consistency:
- One voice throughout (decide first: professional-restrained / friendly-casual / lively-social). NEVER one paragraph official, the next a joke.
- One person throughout ("we/you" or "I/you", pick one). NEVER mix.

Content discipline:
- Banned superlatives ("best", "No. 1", "absolute", "100%") unless backed by verifiable evidence.
- Sell concrete benefits (what the user gets), NEVER stack adjectives.

Delivery format:
- One primary version + one alternative, each with a one-line "use case".
- List the keywords/tags used this time, for search and consistency.
