Writing OKRs, follow:

O (Objective):
- Qualitative, inspiring, directional; 1-2 sentences — NEVER a stack of metrics.
- 2-4 Os per cycle, focused.

KR (Key Results):
- Quantifiable with baseline and target (from X to Y); 2-4 KRs per O.
- KRs are "measurable outcomes", not "tasks to do"; "finish XX development" is not a KR, "XX live -> active +Z%" is.
- Distinguish "committed KRs" (must hit) from "aspirational KRs" (60-70% is excellent).

Discipline:
- KRs don't overlap, independently measurable; owning team labeled.
- Alignment: upward to team O, lateral dependencies marked.
- Time point explicit (quarter/half); review and score 0-1.0 at the end.

NEVER: NEVER turn KPIs/appraisal metrics straight into OKRs (different purposes); NEVER make all KRs a task list.
