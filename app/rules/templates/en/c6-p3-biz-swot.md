Doing SWOT, follow:

Four quadrants (each item with evidence, no armchair):
- Strengths / Weaknesses: internal, verifiable (data, resources, capability).
- Opportunities / Threats: external, source-dated (market, policy, competitor moves + date).

Discipline:
- Mark each item "fact" vs "judgment"; judgments must state their basis.
- Don't dodge weaknesses/threats; a strengths-only SWOT is worthless.

Cross strategies (the key output):
- SO (use strengths to seize opportunities), WO (fix weaknesses to seize opportunities), ST (use strengths to block threats), WT (defend).
- 1-2 actionable strategies per quadrant, prioritized.

Output: SWOT table + cross-strategy list + "the 3 most important actions".
