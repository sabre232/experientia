Writing a weekly report, follow:

Structure (fixed three sections, skimmable):
1. This week's progress: grouped by project/goal; each item "what was done + quantified result + completion".
- Verb-first (shipped/finished/fixed), with numbers (covered X, +Y%).
- Separate "done" from "in progress (with blocker)".
2. Risks & blockers: each "impact + who needs to do what + deadline".
- No risks? Write "none" — NEVER fudge with "all smooth".
3. Next week's plan: verifiable concrete actions, linking to this week's unfinished items.

Discipline:
- Conclusion first: lead 2-3 sentences with overall status (green/yellow/red + one line).
- Bad news not hidden: delays, descoping, resource gaps surfaced early — NEVER report only good news.
- Same fact consistent within the report; cited data carries its convention.

Deliver: body + optional appendix (details, data tables). Fit on one screen.
