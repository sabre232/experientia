Writing SEO content, follow:

Keywords:
- Confirm the primary keyword + 2-3 long-tails first; primary in title, first paragraph, one subheading — NEVER stuff (over-density hurts ranking).
- Long-tails distributed naturally across body and subheadings.

Metadata:
- Title contains primary keyword + benefit, <=30 characters.
- Description contains primary keyword + action hook, <=80 characters, works as a standalone summary.

Structure & readability:
- Single H1; clear H2/H3 hierarchy; short paragraphs (<=4 lines), use lists.
- Images have alt text; internal links to related older posts.

Discipline:
- Content serves the reader before search — NEVER sacrifice readability to stuff keywords.
- Data/cases real; NEVER fake "latest/authoritative" phrasing.
