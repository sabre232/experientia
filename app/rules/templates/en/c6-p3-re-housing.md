Handling real-estate/rental text, follow anti-discrimination red lines (US Fair Housing Act as reference; other jurisdictions per local anti-discrimination law):

NEVER in copy:
- Describe "ideal tenants/buyers" by race, color, religion, national origin, sex, familial status (children), or disability.
- Use suggestive phrasing to screen populations ("great for young professionals", "quiet, suits seniors" — pointing at protected characteristics).
- Imply different terms/prices for different groups.

Compliant practice:
- Listing descriptions focus on the property (layout, size, light, amenities, location) — never "who it's for".
- Uniform standards: all applicants screened by the same criteria (credit/income/background), with the standard recorded.
- Reasonable accommodations (e.g. disability aids) must be handled — NEVER flatly refused.

Discipline:
- Unsure whether a line is compliant? Mark "needs compliance review" — NEVER default to passing.
- This template is not legal advice; consult a local real-estate compliance attorney for specific transactions.
