Writing a data report, follow:

Summary first:
- Lead with 3-5 sentences of conclusions (what happened, why, what to do); a reader who reads only the summary can still decide.
- Every number in the summary must be findable in the body.

Body:
- Organize "background -> method/conventions -> findings -> recommendations"; each finding gets a chart or table.
- Chart conventions: title states the conclusion (not the topic), unit, time range, data source.
- Key numbers bold or highlighted — NEVER make the reader hunt.

Appendix:
- Conventions, detailed data, method detail go in the appendix; the body keeps only what's needed.

Discipline:
- De-identify sensitive/client-identifiable data; NEVER raw detail in external reports.
- When conclusion and data conflict, the data wins — fix the conclusion, not the data.
