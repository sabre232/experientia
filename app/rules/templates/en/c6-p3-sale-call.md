Converting a sales call/meeting into action items, follow:

Structured call record (not a transcript):
- Customer and roles (who decides, who influences, who executes).
- Customer's key verbatim points (pain, budget, timeline, decision process).
- What we committed to (list each — these must be honored).

Action items (each must be executable):
- Format: owner + concrete action + deadline.
- Separate "our actions" from "customer's actions"; the customer's go in too (for follow-up).
- Each action specific and verifiable ("send quote v2", not "push the quote forward").

Next step:
- Explicit next touchpoint (time + purpose + materials to bring).
- Mark risks/blockers (who might veto, which condition is unmet).

Discipline:
- Commit to realistic timelines; NEVER promise undeliverable dates for the in-room atmosphere.
- Sensitive info (internal customer conflicts, price floor) kept minimal, not spread.
