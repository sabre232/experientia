Organizing article structure, follow:

Pyramid principle:
- Conclusion first: lead with the core point/answer, then supporting detail.
- Each level answers the level above; the first sentence of a paragraph is its point.

One idea per paragraph:
- One thing per paragraph; over 5 lines, consider splitting or adding a subheading.
- Parallel content as lists — NEVER chain with "and/also/furthermore".

Logical connections:
- Transitions between paragraphs (cause/contrast/progression) — NEVER flat listing.
- Every point has at least one support (example/data/citation); bare assertions marked "[evidence pending]".

Pre-delivery check:
- Delete off-topic paragraphs; replace "first/next/finally" with more precise logical connectors where they don't fit.
