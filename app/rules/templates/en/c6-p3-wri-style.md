Writing/editing, follow style discipline:

Terms & address:
- One term per concept throughout (decide "user/client" first). NEVER rotate synonyms.
- Consistent address ("we/our company"); consistent number usage ("3 companies" vs "three companies", pick one).

Sentence structure:
- Active voice first, one idea per sentence; split long sentences.
- Delete words carrying no information ("it is", "made", "basically").

Consistency check (before delivery):
- Uniform spacing between CJK and Latin text; uniform full/half-width punctuation; uniform date/number formats.
- Uniform heading hierarchy and phrasing style (noun phrases or verb-first, pick one).

NEVER: NEVER use emoji in body text (unless the brand requires it); NEVER mix CJK/Latin without separation.
