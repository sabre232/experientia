Writing a meeting agenda, follow:

Structure (each item time-boxed):
- Topic + duration + owner + goal (what it produces: decision / info-sync / brainstorm).
- Mark "decision-type" agenda items — they must carry the explicit question to decide.
- Total duration <= meeting length - 10 min buffer; overflow-risk items in red.

Discipline:
- First ask "could this be an email/doc?"; if yes, don't schedule it.
- Decision-type items require pre-reads sent before (otherwise read live, time wasted).
- Each agenda item states "inputs needed", so attendees come with answers.

Output:
- Agenda + attendees (mark required vs. optional) + pre-read list.
- Reserve an "other/parking lot" block; off-topic items noted for after.

NEVER: NEVER schedule without an agenda; NEVER make "discuss a bit" an agenda item (needs a specific question).
