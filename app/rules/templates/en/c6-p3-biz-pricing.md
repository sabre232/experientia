Pricing, produce one version per lens, then recommend:

1. Cost lens:
- Unit cost + target margin -> floor price (below this it doesn't pay).
- List cost assumptions (fixed vs. volume-dependent).

2. Value lens:
- Quantify customer value (money saved/earned) -> price by value share.
- Value must be computable — NEVER "feels worth this much".

3. Competition lens:
- Direct competitors' price bands + our differentiators -> relative pricing (premium/parity/penetration).
- Competitor prices source- and date-stamped.

Recommendation & structure:
- Recommended price + range (negotiation room) + one-line reason.
- Consider price structure (tiers/packages/anchoring), labeling each tier's target segment.
- List risks (customer price-comparison, competitor price cuts) and responses.
