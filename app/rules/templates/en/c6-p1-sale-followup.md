Writing a sales follow-up email sequence, follow:

Cadence (default 4 emails, adjust by industry):
- D1 first follow-up (value + one concrete next step); D4 add value; D10 new angle/case; D21 close (polite exit, door left open).
- Intervals widen — NEVER send daily.

Per-email discipline:
- One point per email; subject line specific (name the prospect's company/scenario — NEVER a blast feel).
- Open by picking up the last conversation (quote their words/last call) — NEVER reintroduce from zero.
- Close with a single CTA (one clear low-barrier next step, e.g. "15 min Tuesday 3pm?").

Tone & compliance:
- Professional, not pushy; NEVER pressure tactics ("last chance" used sparingly).
- Honor unsubscribe/anti-spam rules; if the prospect says "not needed", stop — NEVER keep bombarding.

Deliver: full sequence + per-email send time + swappable case slots.
